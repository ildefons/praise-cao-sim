## Semantics and interpretation rules

1. Throughout the historical I1 track, `sigma_G(A,H;rho)=P(c_G(A,H)>=rho)` concerns the cumulative fraction of **decided** SLA-compliant requests in a trajectory at H (unresolved excluded, zero decisions compliant). It is not automatically an absorbing first-violation time survival function. Later exploratory first-violation scripts represent separate estimands; do not silently fold them into I1 methods.
2. `rho_region` sets which provider's disclosed admissibility region is used. `rho_query` is the required compliant fraction for the trajectory sigma query; historical evaluations sometimes select the same-rho diagonal without making these definitions identical.
3. Provider physical model recovery, provider I1-surface approximation, graph-region boundary construction and graph survival approximation are different inferential targets. The P2-induced-region pilot has a *common deterministic graph boundary* for all three stochastic reconstruction methods.
4. A retained mass over reconstructed models is not a statistically calibrated confidence interval for hidden white-box behavior. Likewise MC precision is not model fidelity.
5. A valid *pre-WB* version change remains a version change, and the after-the-fact Stage-B ablation remains post-hoc. Do not retrospectively call all outcomes of later exploration confirmatory.
6. Pure simulator/benchmark engineering innovations require their own code/provenance audit before novelty claims.

## Ancillary work not yet individually verified

Beyond entries E01–E12, the Phase-5 repository contains exploratory tracks such as `run_graph_conditioned_coupling_pilot_v1.py`, `run_optuna_gp_guarantee_scientific_v2.py` and `run_four_output_survival_validation_v1.py`. These are **distinct experimental targets**, some involving graph-conditioned information or first-violation notions, and should not be conflated with the frozen Phase-5 V3b I1 battery or represented as publication-ready results until a separate execution/result audit. A recent proposed *joint graph parameter optimization* should be inventoried by its exact implementation, frozen inputs and saved output before assigning it a canonical M3 label in B1.

## Source precedence

Per-experiment frozen contract + archived manifest/CSV > checked result checkpoint/closure note > later research narrative > historical manuscript. A registry's `verified` field means that specific saved-table comparison passed: **not** that simulator execution, real-world validity, or all experiment artifacts are certified.
