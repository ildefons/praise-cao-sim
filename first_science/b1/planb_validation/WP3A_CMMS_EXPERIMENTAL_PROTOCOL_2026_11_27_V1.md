## 3. Model and prior (user approved)

Independent per-method coefficient vectors with one intercept, five configuration effects, four pattern effects (10 coefficients). Design row x=(1, onehot(h), onehot(s)), v=mean of all 20 rows. Intercept mean m0=0.50; all factor means zero. Standard deviations tau_alpha=0.20, tau_h=0.12, tau_s=0.08, sigma=0.09; coefficient covariance diagonal; independent homoskedastic residuals. Same prior for every method/policy. Heuristic, not fitted to complete outcomes; midpoint of [0,1], larger intercept variation than either factor, hierarchy and magnitude ratios broadly analogous to PRAISE. Gaussian is working model, not bounded-likelihood model; do not clip latent estimates. Baseline predictive SD sqrt(0.20^2+0.12^2+0.08^2+0.09^2) about 0.2625. Prior selected before acquisition replay, not a claim of blind selection given published source.

Use existing conjugate posterior; latent domain mean m[a]=v^T b[a], q[a]=v^T Sigma[a] v, Delta[a,c]=(v^T Sigma[a] x[c])^2/(sigma^2+x[c]^T Sigma[a] x[c]). KG formula unchanged. Finite posterior mean mtilde retains exact observed losses, uses posterior latent predictions for others. kappa=20*tau_alpha^2+4*tau_h^2+5*tau_s^2 = 0.8896 at base. Proposition 1: mtilde=m0+(1+sigma^2/kappa)(m-m0), common positive slope. KG(mtilde) = same multiplier * KG(m). Use unscaled original m and scores for numerical decisions; do not alter fixed tie tolerance by scaling scores. Verify on synthetic histories and all baseline terminal checkpoints. This applies to shared priors and constant row sums, not arbitrary future covariance.

## 4. Policies, budget and seed design

Retain exactly nine acquisition criteria, dimension-generalized to six methods and twenty conditions:
- Random: uniform over all unused pairs.
- Balanced: maximize -100*n_a-10*n_a,h-2*n_a,s over unused pairs; fixed full catalogue dimension even when a method exhausted.
- Informed: maximize Delta[a,c] times sum over b!=a exp(-0.5*(m[a]-m[b])^2/max(1e-10,q[a]+q[b])).
- Joint KG: maximize one-step Gaussian KG over all unused pairs; d=abs(m[a]-min_{b!=a}m[b]); s=sqrt(Delta); s*phi(d/s)-d*Phi(-d/s) if s>0 else 0; floor tiny negative numerical values at zero as original.
- Two-stage adaptive KG: select method by mean of KG across its remaining conditions, then condition by max KG.
- Two-stage uniform KG: identical method selection, then uniform remaining condition.
- Original joint budgeted Top2: ceil((B-6)/2) extra coverage steps after initialization using Balanced, then restrict available pairs to two lowest current m (stable sort); choose max Delta; if both exhausted use Balanced.
- Two-stage adaptive/uniform Top2: same precommitted coverage phase length; coverage arm score -n_a, condition score -10*n_a,h-2*n_a,s; focus arm score mean Delta among remaining conditions of current two lowest m, condition score Delta. If neither leader has an unused pair, choose among available arms by -n_a and set all their condition scores zero. Adaptive max condition score; uniform uniform remaining condition. This preserves existing controlled Top2 fallback, which is not the joint Top2 Balanced fallback.

All methods evaluated once at common initial condition, six reveals included in B. All 20 possible initial conditions fixed strata, each with 20 seeded replicates per policy. Primary budget grid round(120/84 * original 13 budgets) = (14,21,29,36,43,50,57,64,79,93,100,107,114). Same relative catalogue coverage as PRAISE; no budget chosen from performance. Primary mean regret equally weights these 13 checkpoints (not AUC or cost-weighted). Six budget-independent policies run a single 114-action trajectory for each start/replicate; score all nested prefixes. Three budgeted Top2 variants run separate histories for every B. 400 episodes per policy/budget; 46,800 terminals per matrix/prior setting; 1,242,000 action rows when all histories retained (273,600 nested and 968,400 Top2). No repeat acquisitions.

Canonical policy IDs 0..8: random, balanced, informed, kg_one_step, kg_two_stage_adaptive, kg_two_stage_uniform, budgeted_top2, top2_two_stage_adaptive, top2_two_stage_uniform. Seed root 20261127; NumPy default_rng SeedSequence([root,start_index,replicate,policy_id,stream_id]) for nested; append B before stream_id for Top2, i.e. [root,start_index,replicate,policy_id,B,stream_id]. stream_id=0 joint, 33 arm, 44 condition. Identical seed components across prior settings and matrix sensitivity; independent streams across policies, including controlled Top2 variants (deliberate randomization-design change from original matched Top2 seed family, not a change to selection criteria). Independent seed per B only for budgeted policies. Stable method-major available-pair enumeration, stable shortlist order, uniform among scores >= maximum - 1e-12, exclude exhausted arms, original deterministic argmin(m) recommendation. Define correct selection as selecting any exact minimizer of realized L within absolute 1e-12 (ties); regret=max(0,L[chosen]-min L). Report ties if present. No early stopping or changing repeats after results.

## 5. Endpoints, inference, and interpretation

Primary endpoint: mean terminal simple regret (expected opportunity cost over policy randomization conditional on fixed matrix) over 13 budgets, equal-weight initial conditions, equal-weight 20 repetitions. Secondary: PCS (fraction correct), per-budget regret, PCS, 90th percentile regret, aggregate rank and absolute differences. No pooling PRAISE and CMMS regret on different loss scales, no transfer claim from p-values alone. Plot curves vs evaluations and normalized catalogue fraction. Preserve per-budget outcomes even if nonmonotone.

Primary contrast family (8 prespecified opponents): adaptive two-stage KG minus each of other eight policies in aggregate mean regret; negative favors adaptive. Includes adaptive vs uniform (condition-choice value), vs joint (mean-vs-max arm score), vs Random/Balanced (allocation value), and three Top2 controls plus Informed. Report nominal two-sided 95% and Bonferroni simultaneous 95% familywise intervals (8 x 99.375% individual intervals). Use stratified independent-policy Student-t/Welch-Satterthwaite as current manuscript: within each fixed initial condition, nested policies average each replicate's regret over all budgets first; variance contribution s^2/(20*20^2) per stratum. For budgeted Top2 each stratum-budget independent contribution s^2/(20*20^2*13^2). Contrast variance sum of both policy contributions; df=(sum v_i)^2/sum(v_i^2/19); zero variance reported as conditional observed degeneracy, not population certainty. Nominal per-budget intervals for regret and PCS using same stratified scheme (PCS intervals clipped to [0,1] for display); exploratory PCS contrasts. Supplemental independent-policy stratified bootstrap (10,000 draws for baseline primary; 3,000 sensitivity grid), same within-policy nested replicate resampling across checkpoints, independent across Top2 budgets and policies; fixed start strata. Seed root 20261128 with deterministic per-policy/setting substreams. Not paired across methods' source run_id or unrelated policy streams. All uncertainty conditional on fixed 120-cell matrix, not 376 independent benchmark populations. Report nominal exploratory early seven-budget subset (14..57). No claims of equivalence from crossing zero. If adjusted upper bound <0 support corresponding conditional advantage; crossing zero inconclusive; positive adjusted lower bound supports disadvantage. Descriptive best policy not automatically significant. No go/no-go based on favorable performance.

## 7. Structural sharing diagnostic (RQ2, prespecified secondary)

Nine-policy study alone cannot isolate contribution of the surrogate. Reuse the covariance-control idea from PRAISE manuscript and E1a protocol, now in a bounded adaptive replay (not exhaustive 2^20 masks). At baseline mean and residual variance, define K_B = tau_alpha^2*11^T + tau_h^2*1[h=h'] + tau_s^2*1[s=s']; D=diag(diag(K_B)). Four covariance models: original additive B; independent A=D; interaction C25=.75 K_B+.25 D (same primary interaction share as original E1a); exchangeable E with same diagonal and mean off-diagonal as B (E=(d-r)I+r11^T, d=common diagonal, r=mean off-diagonal). All share marginal prior variance; E also matches total latent mean variance, distinguishes factor-informed sharing from generic pooling. Constant row sums hold for all, but kappa may differ by model; use same unscaled latent scoring within each. Do not fit or change covariance controls after outcomes. Run joint KG and adaptive two-stage KG on each model, same primary matrix, same 13 budgets/400 starts-replicates, same seed components within policy (model not in seed). Reuse additive base results after covariance-space implementation reproduces coefficient-space results; new models 31,200 terminal records total (3 x 2 x 13 x 400). Primary descriptive structural contrast B minus E; B minus A and C25 minus B secondary; report full curves, nominal conditional matched-stream stratified bootstrap intervals within policy across models, preserving budget dependence. If implementation differs numerically at near-ties, diagnose before use and retain both results with documented resolution. No exhaustive fixed mask study; no new model used in main nine-policy comparison. Negative outcomes narrow RQ2 claims. Reference uncertainty study, synthetic interaction regimes and cross-method correlation model deferred to WP5b/WP3b/WP4 respectively.
