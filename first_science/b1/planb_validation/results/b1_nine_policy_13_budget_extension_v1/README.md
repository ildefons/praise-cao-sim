# B1: historical and B0001, nine policies at thirteen budgets

This directory records the final **thirteen-budget comparison** used in the
systems manuscript v0.63. It retains the exact historical and B0001 original
five-budget results and adds eight budgets using the same policy episode
implementations. It does **not** change a predictor, a physical reference, or
the original five-budget archives.

## Design and status

- Budgets: **10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 55, 65, 70, 75, 80**.
- Nine policies: Random, Balanced, Informed, original joint-action Top2,
  original joint-action KG, KG two-stage adaptive/uniform, Top2 two-stage
  adaptive/uniform.
- Each policy/budget/archive: **12 starting cells × 20 seeded repetitions =
  240 episodes**; 2,000 stratified episode-bootstrap draws.
- Episodes per archive: **28,080**, of which 10,800 are the original five-budget
  episodes and 17,280 are the eight additional budgets. Across the two
  archives: **56,160 episodes**. Episodes replay frozen aggregate MAEs;
  they are not independent white-box simulations.
- The historical archive is development evidence. B0001 has newly seeded,
  independently simulated and audited white-box reference trajectories on
  the **same** provider worlds and graphs, not newly sampled environments.
- Original five budgets were frozen before the extension. Eight budgets were
  added after inspecting five-budget B0001 results and should be considered
  **exploratory** rather than a preregistered confirmation test.
- Bootstrap intervals are conditional on each complete 7×12 MAE matrix.
  They do **not** propagate trajectory-level reference uncertainty.

The plots use **exactly the same vertical range, 0 to 0.025 terminal simple
regret**, and the same original policy line/marker assignments. The archived
SVGs are the source figures; the paper's `build.sh` merely converts them to
PDF for pdfLaTeX and does not recalculate data.

The old-five-budget terminal episodes and figures remain independently
available in `../b0001_original_policies_v1/` and
`../controlled_condition_ablation_v1/`; they are **not** overwritten here.
The complete new-eight-budget ZIPs use internal `historical_complete/` and
`b0001_complete/` prefixes, respectively.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, see
`B1_ORIGINAL_NINE_POLICY_13_BUDGET_REPLAY_RUNBOOK_V1.md` and the unchanged
source policy functions in `b1_budget_comparison_v1.py`, `b1_kg_one_step_v1.py`
and `b1_condition_selection_controlled_v1.py`. The extension runner
`extend_b1_original_nine_policy_budgets_v1.py` runs only the added budgets;
`plot_b1_nine_policies_shared_axis_13budgets_v1.py` combines their summaries
with the original five-budget CSVs.

The only compatibility correction at the near-exhaustive budgets sizes
Balanced's scratch coverage array by the fixed seven-method catalogue,
instead of the largest still-available method index. Its scoring and
tie-breaking logic are unchanged. The original policy sources and their
five-budget outputs were not modified.

Paired B0001 thirteen-budget contrasts can be reproduced *without rerunning
any acquisition policy* by invoking
`derive_b1_thirteen_budget_paired_contrasts_v1.py` with this directory and
the original B0001 five-budget replay ZIP. Their intervals are nominal,
unadjusted bootstrap intervals, not new reference-level confidence intervals.
