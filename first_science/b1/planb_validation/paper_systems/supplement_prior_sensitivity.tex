\section*{Scope and replay protocol}
Equation~(5) of the accompanying paper defines the within-method additive prior covariance
\[
\operatorname{Cov}_0(f_a(w,g),f_a(w',g'))=
\tau_\alpha^2+\tau_w^2\mathbf{1}[w=w']+
\tau_g^2\mathbf{1}[g=g'].
\]
The original hyperparameter values are heuristic. We retain the same two frozen
seven-method by twelve-condition reference matrices and thirteen budget limits,
changing only the posterior's numerical hyperparameters. Every variant replays
all nine policies using its own updated Gaussian posterior and acquisition
decisions. The six budget-independent policies preserve their nested budget
prefixes; the three Top2 policies retain budget-specific schedules. Each
archive--scenario--policy--budget cell summarizes 240 terminal episodes.
The baseline replay independently reproduces all 234 paper-figure mean regrets.

The fixed grid covers independent half/double perturbations of the three prior
standard deviations and the residual standard deviation, the world/graph scale
swap, joint half/double scaling of the three prior standard deviations, and two
intercept prior means. Table~\ref{tab:grid} identifies the exact settings.
Each scenario is a complete nine-policy comparison under a shared prior;
the sensitivity analysis does not fit its hyperparameters to a reference
matrix or select a favorable setting for the primary paper.

\section*{Aggregate regret comparisons}
The primary paper averages terminal simple regret equally across the thirteen
budget checkpoints. Under that estimand, Table~\ref{tab:aggregate}
summarizes the number of prior configurations in which adaptive two-stage KG
has strictly lower mean regret than a comparator. The \emph{best Top2}
denotes the lowest thirteen-budget mean among the three Top2 variants
within each archive and prior configuration; it is not a fixed
universal comparator.

The sole configuration where the best Top2 variant has lower average
regret is the historical archive with all three prior scales doubled:
the adaptive-KG excess is $0.0000417$ MAE, about $1.4\%$ of that Top2
regret. The exploratory nominal 95\% bootstrap intervals for
adaptive-minus-uniform exclude zero in 12/14 historical configurations
and 6/14 fresh-reference configurations. These intervals are
\emph{not} corrected across the fourteen-case grid. The primary
manuscript's familywise-adjusted inference applies to the baseline
configuration only.

\section*{Sensitivity by validation budget}
Table~\ref{tab:prior-budget-diagnostics} retains the budget dependence
hidden by the thirteen-budget average. The directional counts in each
archive are ordered as adaptive KG strictly better than, tied with, or
strictly worse than uniform two-stage KG. Prior spread is the
maximum minus the minimum adaptive-KG mean regret across fourteen
configurations, expressed in $10^{-3}$ MAE. The table is descriptive
rather than an additional set of budgetwise significance tests.

On the historical archive, adaptive KG is better than uniform KG in
all fourteen configurations at every checkpoint from $B=15$ to
$B=45$. At $B=55$, adaptive KG has reached zero regret in every
configuration, and from $B=65$ both KG variants tie at zero.
On the fresh-reference archive, the budgetwise direction varies
between configurations at most intermediate checkpoints, and both
KG variants tie at $B=80$. The absolute spread across prior
settings is not monotonic at early and intermediate budgets, but
diminishes to zero or nearly zero as the finite method-selection
task saturates. The acquisition paths need not become identical
when terminal regret does.

\section*{Reproducibility and interpretation}
The accompanying repository contains the scenario-specific
thirteen-budget decision table, exploratory aggregate contrast
table, analysis manifest with source checksums, and the derived
per-budget directional and spread table. The original replay
protocol and executable audit are available at
\url{https://github.com/ildefons/praise-cao-sim/tree/praise-identifiability-feasibility/first_science/b1/planb_validation}.
The analysis establishes sensitivity over this \emph{finite grid},
conditional on two fixed reference matrices. It does not estimate
the optimum covariance hyperparameters, establish absence of
historical human tuning, or compare the additive information-sharing
structure against a distinct surrogate family.
